...

I don't know. Let's find out.

...

```
    1234

    8638     (1234 × 7)

  699678
```

---

A great mathematician isn't great at math because they can multiply huge numbers
in their head. It's because they understand the rules and patterns and systems
behind mathematics.

...

Computer science is the study of rules and patterns and systems.

---

A computer is a bunch of oil and rocks that have been heated up and glued
together. Which doesn't know or understand anything about the world on its own.

...

When you program a computer, you are teaching it to understand rules and
patterns and systems.

...

And if you can teach a bunch of oil and rocks to solve math problems, or play
chess, or predict the weather, then you can teach yourself to do those things.

---

What makes a person smart?

...

Smart people are people who can teach themselves to understand complicated
things.

...

We spend a lot of time in this class learning how to program computers. But the
purpose of this class is not to learn how to program computers. The purpose of
this class is to learn how to understand complicated things by learning to teach
computers how to understand complicated things.
